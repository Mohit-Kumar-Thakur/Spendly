Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Spendly records how you spend your money, so we take the privacy of that information seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have over your own data.

01Data We Collect

We collect the account details you provide when you register, such as your name and email address, together with a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your password in a readable form.

We also store the expense records you choose to enter — the amount, category, date and any description you add. Beyond that, we keep basic technical information such as the time of your last sign-in, which helps us keep your account secure. We do not ask for or store bank account numbers, card details or other payment credentials.

02How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to operate the service and nothing more: authenticating you at sign-in, saving your expenses, and generating the summaries, category breakdowns and date-range views that make the app useful.

We do not sell your personal or financial information, and we do not use your expense records to build advertising profiles or share them with advertisers. We may use aggregated, anonymised statistics that cannot identify you to understand how the service is performing.

03Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database and kept for as long as your account remains active. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your associated records.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, including password hashing and restricted access to the underlying data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

04Third Party Services

Spendly keeps external dependencies to a minimum. The application loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch those files; that request is handled under Google's own privacy policy and carries no expense data.

We do not integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics trackers, and we do not pass your expense records to any outside service. If we ever rely on a hosting or infrastructure provider to run the application, they may process data strictly on our behalf and only as needed to keep the service running. We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so.

05Your Choices

You can review and update your account details from within the application, correct or remove any expense you have logged, and close your account if you no longer wish to use Spendly. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it.

06Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date shown at the top of this page, and significant changes may also be communicated within the application.

07Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about how your information is handled, please get in touch through the contact details provided in the application. Our Terms and Conditions cover the rest of your relationship with Spendly.
